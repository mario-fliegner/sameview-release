SameView – Google Play Category & Tags Recommendation (EN)

Research date: 2026-07-24

==================================================
IMPORTANT NOTE
==================================================

Only tags that are officially selectable in the Google Play Console
("Store presence > Store listing > Categorization > Manage tags") may be
used for this app. No custom, invented, or freeform keywords are permitted
here (freeform ASO keywords are tracked separately in Keywords_Research_EN.txt
and are NOT the same thing as Play Console tags).

Google does not publish a single canonical public list of all available
tags outside the Play Console UI itself. The tag names below were
cross-checked against multiple third-party ASO sources that document the
current Play Console tag picker (see SOURCES section). Before final
submission, re-verify tag availability live inside Play Console, since
Google can add, rename, or retire tags over time.

==================================================
RECOMMENDED CATEGORY
==================================================

Photography

Justification:
SameView's core function is capturing a new photo through a live camera
preview with a reference-photo overlay, and comparing the result against
that reference. This is a camera/photo-capture-centric app, not a general
utility, video, or lifestyle app. "Photography" is one of Google Play's
official app categories and is the closest match to this core functionality.
Source: FullDescription_EN.txt ("Choose a reference photo and capture the
same scene... reference photo overlay on the camera preview...").

==================================================
RECOMMENDED TAGS (max. 3 of 5 available slots used)
==================================================

1. Camera
   Justification: The app's primary interaction is a live camera screen
   with a reference-photo overlay used to recapture a scene. This is
   confirmed as an officially available Play Console tag.
   Source: FullDescription_EN.txt, feature list ("Adjustable reference
   photo overlay with alignment markers").

2. Photography
   Justification: Matches the recommended primary category and the app's
   overall positioning around capturing, comparing, and sharing photos.
   Confirmed as an officially available Play Console tag.
   Source: FullDescription_EN.txt (overall app description).

3. Travel
   Justification: One of the app's explicitly documented use cases is
   revisiting favorite places and travel destinations to recreate a
   photo from the same spot (supported by the optional GPS "recreation
   guidance" feature). Confirmed as an officially available Play Console
   tag.
   Source: FullDescription_EN.txt ("Whether it's your favorite places,
   your travels, nature, architecture, or family photos..."); optional
   GPS guidance documented in Technical_DataSafety_Audit.md.

==================================================
TAGS CONSIDERED AND EXPLICITLY REJECTED
==================================================

- "Photo editor"
  Rejected because the app explicitly does NOT position itself as a photo
  editor. This is a documented ASO decision, not a new judgment made here.
  Source: Keywords_Research_EN.txt, "KEYWORDS TO AVOID" / "Avoid
  positioning as: photo editor".

- "Video editing"
  Rejected because video export in SameView is a single fixed comparison
  export (no manual editing capability), so the tag would not be
  "obviously relevant" per Google's own tag guidance. Source: Play
  Console Help (see SOURCES) and FullDescription_EN.txt ("Create photos
  and videos to share" — export only, no editing tools described).
